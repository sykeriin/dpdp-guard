## Version A (short)
Today the DPDP Consent Manager registration window opens. Most of the Act's obligations (notice, consent, security, breach reporting, data-principal rights) are due 13 May 2027, about six months out.

Most people are thinking about websites and apps. Almost nobody is thinking about the WhatsApp and voice bots that already collect names, numbers and addresses from customers, and that agencies deploy by the dozen.

A bot can't satisfy "free, specific, informed consent" with a line in the terms of service. It has to ask, per purpose, inside the chat, and be able to delete the data from the CRM, the vector DB and the logs when someone says "meri details hatao".

I'm building a small layer for that (consent in chat, masking, erasure across stores, audit export) and I'm looking for 20 people who build or run bots for clients to tell me what they're doing about it. Not selling anything yet.
