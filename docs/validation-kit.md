## Who to talk to
Agencies/freelancers building WhatsApp & voice bots (LinkedIn, Upwork, Fiverr "WhatsApp chatbot India"), in-house devs at D2C brands, clinics, coaching institutes, small fintechs. List 40, book 20.

## Interview script (20 min, don't pitch first)
1. How many client bots do you run? On what stack (LangGraph, n8n, raw WhatsApp Cloud API, a vendor platform)?
2. What personal data do those bots collect? Where is it stored (CRM, sheets, vector DB, transcripts)?
3. Have clients asked about DPDP? What did you tell them? What are you planning before May 2027?
4. Who would own a data-deletion request today? Walk me through the last time someone asked to delete data.
5. Which tools have you looked at? (Write down every name. If the same one keeps coming up, that's your competitor.)
6. Demo: run `examples/e2e_demo.py` or the bot checker on one of their transcripts.
7. What would this have to do for you to pay for it? What would you pay per client bot per month? Would you resell it?
8. Ask for a letter of intent (3 needed) and one intro.

Record: name, stack, # bots, would-pay (Y/N), price quoted, objections, competitor named.
